Privacy policy

Last updated 30 September 2026. This policy covers the Google Play edition of Meow Assistant, package com.meow.meowmba, published by dekayrekords.

Meow Assistant stores your workspace on your phone and lets you connect optional AI providers, Computer workspaces and other services. You do not need a Meow account. Choosing a cloud feature can send its inputs and relevant context to the selected service. Local storage does not mean that every feature operates entirely on your phone.

Data stored on your phone

Local records include conversations, memories, notes, workspace files, settings, downloaded models and enrolled speaker profiles. Relevant conversation history, memories or files can become part of a request to a provider you configure. Credentials are stored using the app's encrypted storage and are used to authenticate with their intended service. App data is excluded from automatic Android cloud backup. Files you export or share create separate copies at the destination you choose.

Optional services and data they receive

  • Cloud conversations and connected tasks: your request and relevant history, memories, tool results or documents can be sent to your selected provider or Computer/server endpoint. This can include names, contact details, account identifiers, preferences, drafts, calendar information and app or browsing context that you supply or enable for a task.
  • Speech: a configured cloud transcription service receives recorded speech. Cloud speech output receives the text to speak. The device's system speech service may also process recordings under its provider's policy. Choosing an on-device chat model does not by itself make a separately selected speech service local.
  • Vision: a configured cloud vision service can receive selected images, camera frames or screen captures, with relevant instructions. On-device vision processing remains local, apart from the SDK diagnostics described below.
  • Media generation: the selected service receives prompts and any supported reference inputs. Providers may include Cloudflare Workers AI, AI Horde or a configured image, music or video server. AI Horde community workers receive submitted prompts; anonymous use includes the dataset-sharing disclosure shown in the app. With a personal key, the app requests no dataset sharing, but workers still receive the prompt.
  • Location, navigation, weather and search: enabled features may send a query and relevant approximate or precise location to the selected source. Directions opened in another app are also handled under that app's policy.
  • Downloads and connections: model hosts and connected services receive requests and any authentication needed for them. Network services can receive ordinary connection information, including an IP address. Providers can include OpenAI, OpenRouter, Google Gemini, BigPickle, Hugging Face or a compatible endpoint you enter. Availability depends on your configuration.

These services apply their own policies to retention, security, training and deletion. Selecting a different provider affects future requests and does not erase earlier submissions. Review the selected provider's terms before sending private content. Meow does not sell your personal data or include advertising SDKs.

Included SDKs and diagnostics

Google ML Kit and related Firebase configuration/installation components can collect device and app information, device or installation identifiers, feature events, configuration information, error codes and performance measurements for diagnostics and usage analytics. This is separate from where your AI conversations are processed. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and Firebase's data disclosure.

The optional Meta wearable integration uses Meta's Device Access Toolkit. Meow sets the SDK's analytics opt-out flag. Enabled account/device registration and wearable services are still subject to Meta's privacy policy; the flag does not change the separate Meta companion app's practices.

Meow's support-report action is optional. It includes app/Android version, available memory or thermal status, operating-mode switches and a limited set of process-exit codes/timestamps. It does not include your chat, recordings, credentials or raw logs. Android's share chooser lets you choose its destination. Included SDK diagnostics have separate behavior; this support-report switch does not disable them.

Permissions and controls

Microphone, camera and screen capture support features you enable. Fresh setup starts with the microphone off; skipping or denying sensor setup leaves the microphone and camera off. Location supports enabled navigation, road and nearby-place features. Calendar access supports enabled calendar tasks. Bluetooth supports optional accessories. Notification access supports enabled road alerts; notifications provide service and download status. Optional media and usage access support the corresponding file or app-management features. You can change feature switches in the app and revoke permissions in Android Settings.

The Play edition does not include autonomous Accessibility control of other apps or physical robot actuation. Permissions alone do not make a cloud feature local: also check the chosen speech, chat and vision providers.

Connections and security

Public API endpoints are required to use HTTPS by the app. SSH connections use SSH encryption. User-configured local-network endpoints can use HTTP, which is not encrypted in transit. A universal claim that every connection is encrypted would therefore be incorrect. The destination's operator controls how it stores and handles data it receives.

Retention and deletion

Delete supported local conversations and records using the app's controls. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes its local data, credentials and downloaded assets. Preserve anything you need before doing so. Exported, shared or manually copied files must be removed separately from their destinations.

Removing local data does not delete information already sent to an AI provider, SDK operator, connected server or sharing destination. Request deletion from the relevant provider or destination administrator under its policy. Meow does not offer a single mechanism that deletes all third-party copies, and does not promise automatic deletion within a fixed period.

Children

Meow Assistant is not directed at children under 13.
